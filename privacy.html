Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 11, 2026

BatterUp is an iOS app for volunteer coaches of youth softball teams. This policy explains exactly what data the app and this website handle. The short version: BatterUp does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no user accounts, no servers, and no third-party SDKs.

What data BatterUp collects

None. BatterUp does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you, your players, or anyone else. The app has no user accounts, no login, and no backend servers. It does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Data stored on your device and in your iCloud

Information you enter into BatterUp — including team rosters, player names and numbers, game records, lineups, at-bats, and scores — is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad using Apple’s on-device SwiftData storage. If you are signed into iCloud, BatterUp also syncs this data to your own private iCloud database so it appears on your other Apple devices and can be restored after wiping or replacing a device. Your private iCloud database is only accessible by you — BatterUp’s developer never sees it. If you delete the app, the local copy is removed along with it.

Children’s privacy

BatterUp is designed to be used by adults — coaches and team managers — to record games and player performance for youth softball teams. While children’s names, jersey numbers, and game statistics may appear in coach-entered rosters, none of this information is transmitted off the device by BatterUp. The app does not create accounts for children, does not communicate with any servers, and does not share children’s data with any third party.

Sharing

BatterUp uses Apple’s standard ShareLink to let coaches share game recaps and similar content. Sharing is always initiated by the coach, the content is composed locally on the device, and the coach decides what to send and to whom (for example, by Messages, Mail, or another app of their choice). BatterUp itself does not transmit shared content to any server.

Analytics, tracking, and cookies

BatterUp does not use analytics or crash reporting from any third party. The app does not integrate with any social networks or advertising networks. This website does not set cookies and does not include any tracking scripts or third-party analytics.

Third-party services

BatterUp does not integrate with any third-party services, advertising networks, or social platforms.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to support@batterupscorebook.com.
