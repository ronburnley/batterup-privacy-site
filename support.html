Frequently asked questions

Where is my game data stored?
Locally on your device. BatterUp does not have servers and does not upload your data anywhere.
What happens if I lose my phone?
Game data is currently device-local, so it would not be recoverable. iCloud sync is planned for a future version.
Does BatterUp work without internet?
Yes. BatterUp is fully functional offline. There is nothing to sign in to and no network connection required.
Can I export season stats?
Exporting season stats is planned but not yet available.
